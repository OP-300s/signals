Backup API (optional)

Used when Twelve Data fails or hits its limit, before Binance. Paste an API address that returns candles, with your key inside the address. Use a different service, not another Twelve Data account. You can use {tf} for 15m, 30m, 1h or 4h, {tfd} for 15min, 30min, 1h or 4h, {min} for 15, 30, 60 or 240, {limit} for the number of candles, and {from} and {to} for start and end time in seconds.

Paper market

Paper bot strategy

Indicators: the old score from the trend, momentum and other indicators. Candlestick: only confirmed candlestick patterns. Both: the bot follows a confirmed candlestick pattern, and the indicators when there is none. The paper bot, your alerts and the past results use this strategy. The Buy, Sell or Wait at the top and "Why the bot says this" always show the indicators. Candlestick calls are rare, because a pattern needs a key level and a confirmation candle.

Server (optional)

Let my laptop bot copy the paper trades

Off by default. When on, the server shares the open paper trades with the bot program on your laptop. Turn it off and tap Save to stop any new real trades.

With a server address, paper trading and notifications run on your Cloudflare server and the Paper market card shows its results. The password is your ntfy topic. It is used to send your settings to the server and to reset the paper account. Leave both empty to keep everything on this phone.
